# Roadmap

Estimates are rough effort in hours. Status values: **Planned**, **In progress**, **Done**. Completed phases are marked **Done**; everything else is planned.

## Audience and priorities

SpecGarage is aimed mainly at Argentine single-make (_monomarca_) categories. That drives three choices: Spanish becomes a first-class language soon after the MVP (Phase 8), numbers follow the Argentine convention of a decimal comma and dot thousands separator, and the data model follows how those regulations are organized (technical regulation, sporting regulation, official annexes, per-event particular regulation, lettered sub-articles).

## MVP — v0.1.0 (35 h)

Smallest useful product: import a category's rules from a file, start an inspection for a vehicle at an event, record measurements, verdicts, and evidence on one checklist screen, and see what is compliant, non-compliant, and still open.

MVP limits, stated up front: English interface only; numbers need a dot as the decimal separator and anything ambiguous is rejected; measurement units must match the rule's unit (no conversion); only numeric limits and manual verdicts; no editor UI, exports, or offline exchange.

## Considered, not scheduled

- Parts, seals, and tire ledger (serial numbers, usage counts, seal numbers per vehicle). Tire limits are served by declared counts in Phase 10; a ledger would fit after Phase 13.
- Loading a real category's published regulations as a bundled example. This is a data-only change but needs the category organizer's permission.

## Excluded

Official certification or homologation, license validation, personal technical passports, inspector or driver identification, OCR, AI interpretation of regulations, image recognition, telemetry and timing, cloud sync, phone and tablet use.
